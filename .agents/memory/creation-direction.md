The creator wants MANIFEST to be "ever so ethereal" and smarter in both its appearance and generated ideas, aiming to "set a new standard for ... creation and innovations."

**Why:** The creator explicitly chose both visual refinement and generation quality, rather than either alone.

**How to apply:** Keep the atmosphere subtle and the content concrete. Improve originality through useful mechanisms and credible experiments, not claims of guaranteed novelty, success or ownership.

Random creative angles are inspiration for broad input, not authority to override explicit products or add unnecessary business models.

**Why:** A live check of a repairable light adopted an unnecessary monthly parts subscription to satisfy a random angle despite the creator's specific product request. Surprise alone is not a better solution.

**How to apply:** Preserve explicit product, buyer and constraints across generation, refinement and selected Mix handoff. Test the riskiest assumption early; do not confuse paid demand with compliments or signups.

Treat prompt standards as guidance, not verified output guarantees.

**Why:** Even stronger-reasoning live checks returned unsupported certification wording, delayed safety review and refundable deposits described as first revenue. Valid JSON and passing prompt-contract tests did not establish factual or safety quality.

**How to apply:** Describe generation improvements honestly. Evaluate representative outputs for semantic quality before making safety, novelty or evidence claims; retain latency checks alongside quality checks.
