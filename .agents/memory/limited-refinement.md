---
name: Limited refinement
description: Product reason for limiting follow-up context to one opportunity
---

For ordinary visitors, keep MANIFEST's generation interaction constrained: show the initial result, then offer one optional chance to add context or correct it, not a multi-turn chat. The verified app owner is explicitly exempt from both generation rate limits and the one-refinement limit.

**Why:** The creator explicitly chose to respond after seeing the first result and wanted to avoid giving users too many choices. They later clarified that these limits should apply to everybody else, not to them, and chose account sign-in to identify their privileged access.

**How to apply:** Preserve ordinary visitors' limits when changing the generation flow. A venture's anonymous creator identity is not proof of app-owner privilege. Owner exemptions must require verified, explicitly designated account identity; never grant them to all venture creators or the first person who signs up. Quantity exemptions do not bypass privacy or concurrent-work safeguards.
